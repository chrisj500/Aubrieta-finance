# Verbena Finance

Verbena is a private, household-focused personal finance platform designed to recreate and improve on the most useful capabilities of classic Mint while remaining provider-neutral and self-hosted.

## Core goals

- Connect real bank, credit-card, mortgage, loan, and investment accounts.
- Avoid lock-in to any one financial-data provider.
- Detect recurring bills and subscriptions automatically.
- Use authoritative liability data for credit-card and mortgage due dates when available.
- Notify household members before bills are due.
- Track budgets, goals, cash flow, net worth, investments, and trends.
- Preserve historical data even when an account changes aggregation providers.
- Keep registration private to the household.

## Foundation

Verbena is being built from a pinned snapshot of the MIT-licensed [Open Finance](https://github.com/DeseretSaint/open-finance) project, with substantial changes to aggregation, bills, household identity, and product experience.

See [ARCHITECTURE.md](ARCHITECTURE.md) and [UPSTREAM.md](UPSTREAM.md).

## Status

Early foundation work. Do not use with production financial credentials until the security and data-migration milestones in the roadmap are complete.
